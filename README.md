A flat, lightweight 1024×1024 news infographic covering six real stories
from Thursday 19 November 2026: a hero panel for the top story, a dotted
world map with numbered pins, colour-coded story cards with a key stat on
each, and every headline reporting the outcome — not the preview.

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119

## Stories (all verified against primary sources, 19 November 2026)

1. **Trump, Xi & Putin hold first-ever trilateral talks** — 90 minutes at
   APEC in Shenzhen; a four-point joint statement, no deal on Ukraine.
   Primary source: USA Today, 19 Nov 2026.
2. **World Court finds Myanmar guilty** — ICJ rules 9–6 in The Gambia v.
   Myanmar over the 2017 Rohingya campaign. Primary source: IIMM
   (UN Independent Investigative Mechanism for Myanmar), 19 Nov 2026.
3. **Suneung day amid AI-cheating** — 551,864 students sit South Korea's
   nine-hour exam; 98 caught with phones. Primary source: AJP
   (Asia JoongAng Press), 19 Nov 2026.
4. **Akvan nears India's east coast** — IMD red alert; landfall near
   Gopalpur by noon Friday. Primary source: India Meteorological
   Department, National Bulletin No. 29, 0900 IST 19 Nov 2026.
5. **Anthropic's record IPO soars, then settles** — ANT opens at $111.50,
   closes at $97.17 (+17%) after the $81.3bn raise, the largest IPO on
   record. Primary source: Nasdaq quote, trading day 19 Nov 2026.
6. **GTA VI is out — and already breaking records** — Leonida launches at
   midnight worldwide; first reviews score 99/100 as 2.3 million watch
   on Twitch. Primary source: Le Monde, 19 Nov 2026; Rockstar Games
   (release date set 6 Nov 2025).

## Files

- `entry.png` — the infographic (1024×1024, 127,797 bytes).
- `main.py` — stdlib-only script that copies `entry.png` to
  `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` and verifies the copy by size.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 -P -s main.py
```

## The infographic

A single 1024×1024 image that sums up the day in six stories. The biggest
story gets a wide hero panel at the top, a dotted world map sits underneath
with numbered pins marking where each story happened, and below that six
colour-coded cards carry one headline and one key stat each. Every headline
reports what actually happened, not what was promised.

## The six stories

1. **Trilateral first.** Trump, Xi and Putin sit down together for the first
   time — 90 minutes of talks at APEC in Shenzhen — and leave with a
   four-point joint statement, but no deal on Ukraine.
   *Source: USA Today, 19 Nov 2026.*
2. **ICJ verdict.** The International Court of Justice rules 9–6 in The
   Gambia v. Myanmar over the 2017 campaign against the Rohingya.
   *Source: IIMM (UN Independent Investigative Mechanism for Myanmar),
   19 Nov 2026.*
3. **Suneung day.** 551,864 students sit South Korea's nine-hour college
   entrance exam; 98 of them are caught with phones.
   *Source: AJP (Asia JoongAng Press), 19 Nov 2026.*
4. **Cyclone Akvan.** The storm closes on India's east coast under an IMD red
   alert, with landfall expected near Gopalpur by noon Friday and winds
   reaching 110 km/h.
   *Source: India Meteorological Department, National Bulletin No. 29,
   0900 IST 19 Nov 2026.*
5. **Anthropic IPO.** ANT opens at $111.50 and closes at $97.17, up 17% on the
   day, after a $81.3bn raise — the largest IPO on record.
   *Source: Nasdaq quote, trading day 19 Nov 2026.*
6. **GTA VI is out.** The game launches worldwide at midnight in Leonida;
   early reviews sit at 99/100 and 2.3 million people watch on Twitch.
   *Sources: Le Monde, 19 Nov 2026; Rockstar Games (release date set
   6 Nov 2025).*
